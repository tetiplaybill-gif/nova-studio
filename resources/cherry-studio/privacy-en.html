NOVA Studio Privacy Policy

Updated: November 17, 2026
Effective: November 17, 2026

NOVA Studio ("the Software") is the desktop client for cdkai.cc, a modified version of the open-source project Cherry Studio. This policy explains how the Software handles your information. Your cdkai.cc account and the NOVA API are also covered by the cdkai.cc privacy notice.

1. Data kept on your computer

Conversations, assistants, knowledge bases, files, settings and API keys are stored in the Software's data folder on your computer. They are not uploaded to cdkai.cc or anywhere else. Uninstalling the Software or deleting its data folder removes them.

2. Data sent to cdkai.cc

  • Sign-in: the email, password and code you enter in the NOVA account card are sent only to cdkai.cc to sign you in. The resulting session is stored encrypted with your system keychain; where no keychain is available it is kept in memory only, and you sign in again after quitting.
  • API requests: when you chat with a model, your prompts, attachments and the model's replies pass through cdkai.cc to the model provider. cdkai.cc does not store their content; it records request metadata (time, key, model, usage, cost and status code) for billing and troubleshooting and deletes it within 180 days.
  • Balance and key: the Software reads your balance and creates one API key named "NOVA Studio" for this computer, which is deleted when you sign out.

3. What the Software does not collect

The Software sends no usage analytics, crash reports, device identifiers or announcement requests, and does not connect to any service run by the Cherry Studio team.

4. Third-party services you turn on

If you turn on web search, MCP servers, mini apps, cloud backup (such as WebDAV or S3) or note sync (such as Notion or SiYuan), the related data is sent to that service as you configure it, under its own privacy policy. None of these connect on their own.

5. Model providers

Model providers may process the content you send through the API under their own policies. Please don't include sensitive personal information you don't need to share.

6. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the Software asks you to read the new version the next time it starts.

7. Contact

To view, correct or delete your account information, contact us through the support channel on cdkai.cc.
